# User Journey

**1. Ralph meets Faustin.**  
Ralph, a college student, meets Faustin, the founder of an AI-chip startup, at a hackathon. Ralph likes the company, but he is not looking for a job yet. Before leaving, he opens Circle and records a short note about Faustin using the **Remember Someone** screen.

**2. Circle remembers the context.**  
Circle turns Ralph’s note into connections between Faustin, AI hardware, startups, and systems engineering. Ralph does not need to organize anything himself. He simply saves the moment and moves on.

**3. Ralph’s goal changes.**  
Several months later, Ralph starts looking for a full-time job. On the **Current Goal** screen, he writes: *“I want to work as a systems engineer at an early-stage AI startup.”*

**4. Circle brings Faustin back.**  
Circle notices that Ralph’s new goal connects to the old note about Faustin and resurfaces him on the **Rediscover** screen. Ralph sees why Faustin is relevant, remembers their conversation, and decides to reach out.

Without Circle, Ralph would likely have forgotten a useful weak connection. Circle makes sure he does not.
