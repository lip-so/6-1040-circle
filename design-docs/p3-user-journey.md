# P3: Beta user journey

After meeting Mira at a campus event, Ralph signs into Circle through Commons and saves an encounter using text, voice or a form. Circle organizes the note into a contact card, preserving his original words. Later, he asks Find, “Who works on solar panels?” Circle returns Mira from his own saved contacts with supporting details. He expands her card and opens Personal notes to recall the conversation. If details need correcting, he edits the prefilled form and saves. To remove the encounter, he clicks the red basket and confirms deletion.
