## Before a session

- Arrange an individual Commons account with access to the app. Check the public app and sign-in independently before inviting a participant; do not assume course access is automatic for everyone.
- Use an account containing only the synthetic fixture set for the exercise, or clearly distinguish the new fixtures from existing records. Preserve any existing personal notes. Participants enter [the four fixtures](synthetic-encounters.md) through the real app, not the database.
- Check the tested source/deployment revision, browser/device, connection and sign-in. Keep an actual microphone exercise optional until permissions/hardware have been rehearsed; the three task cards do not depend on it.
- Explain: “Circle organizes notes, searches and transcribes with an external AI service. Saved notes are scoped to your signed-in account. We will use made-up people and contacts. An AI interpretation can be wrong. Removing a note clears the app’s encounter/evidence; we cannot promise deletion from every external service record.”
- Obtain any study consent separately. Keep participant identity/consent, completed forms and recordings outside the public repository. Use a local anonymous session code on the [blank observation sheet](observation-sheet.md) / [one-page PDF](../../output/pdf/p4-observation-sheet.pdf). Do not collect passwords or access codes.

## Short rehearsal brief for Sofia

Allow about 15–20 minutes for access, fixture entry and the three tasks, then adjust after a real rehearsal. First run the kit yourself against the deployed release. Confirm the two Mira encounters stay separate, the battery-work result has source support, and the discussion-only note does not establish expertise. That rehearsal is not participant research.

Give one [task card](task-cards.md) at a time. Read its outcome without naming a control or demonstrating a route. Ask the participant to say what they expect and what supports their decision. Stay quiet during hesitation; record the visible action and time before interpreting it. If stuck, record when and what help you give. Separate failure to obtain Commons access from difficulty using the app.

After each task ask: “What makes you confident this worked?” and “Was anything different from what you expected?” At the end ask what was hardest and what they would expect the app to remember next time. Do not suggest the answer. Leave every observation blank until a session actually occurs.

Fixtures and resulting notes are disposable for this exercise, but cleanup must be a deliberate participant/facilitator action. Never remove existing personal records to prepare an empty state. Record actual results and limitations before revising the design summary or claiming testing readiness in a submission.
