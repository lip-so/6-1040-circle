# Three participant task cards

Give one card at a time, after fixture setup. These cards describe outcomes, not controls or click sequences. Do not show facilitator expectations during the task.

## Card 1 — keep useful meeting context

You have just met Quinn Zhao at a coastal mapping workshop. Quinn maps accessible paths along the coast, and you talked about ocean sensors. Their fictional contact is `quinn.maps@example.test`. You promised to send the café map. Keep enough context that you could recover who this was and what you promised next week. Decide when you are satisfied it is retained.

## Card 2 — find someone for a later need

A student club now needs help designing a battery for an outdoor project. Use the encounters you have kept to decide whether anyone might help. Explain which saved information supports your decision and how certain you are. If no saved information is sufficient, say so.

## Card 3 — correct the record and remove a mistake

You learned that the Mira from the energy meetup develops acoustic sensors now; the solar-panel/battery activity in your saved account is outdated. Correct that information while keeping the meeting context, contact and promise you still need. You also realize the separate design-workshop Mira encounter was entered by mistake and should no longer be kept. Finish with a record you trust, and explain what should be searchable and what first-saved wording, if any, should remain visible.

## Facilitator checks after the participant finishes

Do not reveal these as instructions: Card 1 should retain meaningful context and the promise only after deliberate save. Card 2 should distinguish direct battery work from the equal-name/discussion distractors using evidence. Card 3 should change the current activity without losing unrelated information, leave first wording clearly historical, remove only the design-workshop encounter and retain the energy-meetup Mira as a separate identity. Record actual interpretation and recovery; these are expected outcomes, not claimed results.
